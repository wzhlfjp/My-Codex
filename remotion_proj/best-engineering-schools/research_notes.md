# Research Notes

## Scope

Project: "America's Best Engineering Schools"

Ranking type: U.S. News Best Engineering Schools / graduate engineering rankings. Years are displayed as the ranking edition year when the source identifies an edition, or as the publication year of the cited ranking table/article.

## Source Hierarchy

Primary target source: U.S. News.

Sources used:

- U.S. News 2003 Graduate School Rankings: Engineering page, archived in a University of Washington mirror.
- U.S. News 2024 Best Engineering Schools press release, used only for the top five rows it verifies.
- U.S. News 2026 Best Graduate Schools press release, used only for the top three rows it verifies.
- Yahoo republication of a U.S. News Education top-20 engineering graduate programs article for 2020.
- University-list archive pages labeled as U.S. News graduate engineering rankings for 2015, 2017, and 2018.
- Public reposts labeled as U.S. News engineering rankings for 2007-2012, 2016, 2019, 2021-2023, and 2025.
- Institutional 2026 U.S. News rank announcements from Texas A&M and the University of Maryland.
- Higher-education/news summaries used when direct U.S. News tables were inaccessible, including 2026 rows beyond the official top three.

## Data Coverage

The normalized animation dataset includes a timeline object for every year 2000-2026.

Rows are included only when a source-backed ranking row was available. The current dataset has sourced rows for 2003, 2007-2012, 2015-2026. Some of those years are partial because the accessible source verifies fewer than 20 rows. The 2026 rows now combine row-level sources: U.S. News/PRNewswire for ranks 1-3, a higher-education news summary for ranks 4-10, and official university releases for Texas A&M (#14) and Maryland (#18).

The years 2000-2002, 2004-2006, 2013, and 2014 are explicitly marked as source gaps. No rankings are fabricated, estimated, carried forward, or interpolated for those years.

## Animation Data Scope

The animation is ranking-only.

Rows show:

- U.S. News rank
- standardized university name
- movement versus the previous verified ranking year

No score, acceptance rate, enrollment, tuition, salary, research spending, test-score, GPA, or other non-ranking quantitative fields are used in the generated animation dataset.

## Ambiguities And Decisions

- Ties are represented when multiple schools share the same numeric rank in a source table.
- Source tables occasionally omit the repeated rank number on subsequent tied rows. The raw data stores those schools with the shared rank when the source context indicates a tie.
- University names are standardized across years. For example, engineering-school labels are reduced to the parent university name, such as `Stanford University`.
- Movement indicators compare each visible row with the previous verified ranking year that has rows, matching the template behavior.
- Each year in the 2000-2026 animation timeline displays for 1.5 seconds, the same timing as `best-law-schools`.
- The source mapping file `data/processed/source_mapping.csv` is generated by the build script and records source IDs and URLs at the school-year-field level.
- Source metadata includes source URL, source type, confidence level, and notes. Individual ranking rows may override a year-level source when a partial year is assembled from multiple verified sources.

## Follow-Up To Improve Accuracy

- Replace secondary reposts with direct U.S. News exports, archived pages, library database captures, or scans where available.
- Fill source-gap years only when a complete U.S. News top-20 engineering ranking table is found.
- Re-audit 2024 and 2026 if full U.S. News top-20 rows become accessible; the current rows for those years are intentionally partial.
