# Research Notes

## Scope

Project: "U.S. News Best Business Schools: Top Full-Time MBA Programs, 2000-2026"

Ranking type: U.S. News full-time MBA / Best Business Schools rankings. Years are publication years, consistent with the convention noted by Poets&Quants.

## Source Hierarchy

Primary target source: U.S. News.

Practical limitation: U.S. News current ranking pages are not reliably crawlable in this workspace and U.S. News does not openly expose historical ranking tables. Poets&Quants notes this limitation in its historical MBA ranking article.

Sources used:

- Poets&Quants historical table for 2001-2012, reported as U.S. News rankings.
- Poets&Quants 2026 U.S. News article for 2020-2026 rank history for the recent top 25.
- Public Infogram "US News MBA Rankings 1990-2021" linked from a Reddit post for early and middle historical rank continuity. This is marked `medium_low` confidence in the raw data and should be replaced if official scans or archived U.S. News tables are obtained.
- Wayback capture of the original MBA50 article "A History of the US News MBA Ranking 1990-2013" for publication-cadence documentation. This source says U.S. News initially published every two years and moved to annual results from 1994 onward; its table columns are 1990, 1992, then 1994 onward.
- Banker.az mirror of the MBA50 historical U.S. News MBA ranking article for publication-cadence documentation. The article states U.S. News MBA rankings were published every two years before becoming annual from 1994 onward; its chart labels skip 1991 and 1993.

## Data Coverage

The normalized animation dataset includes a timeline object for every year 2000-2026.

Ranking rows are available for:

- 2000-2026.

The current animation covers 2000-2026 because earlier historical data were incomplete. Years before 2000 remain in the raw research file where available, but are outside the generated animation timeline and validation window.

## Animation Data Scope

The animation is ranking-only.

Rows show:

- U.S. News rank
- standardized school name
- movement versus the previous verified ranking year

No bar-length metric, GMAT, salary, employment, acceptance-rate, GPA, GRE, or other non-ranking quantitative fields are used in the generated animation dataset.

When ties cross the #20 cutoff, all schools ranked #20 or better are retained so tied ranks are not split. This means some years can display slightly more than 20 rows.

## Ambiguities And Decisions

- Ties are inferred when multiple schools share the same numeric rank in a given source row.
- School names are standardized to stable keys such as `wharton`, `chicago_booth`, and `berkeley_haas`.
- Each year in the 2000-2026 animation timeline displays for 1.5 seconds.
- The source mapping file `data/processed/source_mapping.csv` is generated by the build script and records source IDs and URLs at the school-year-field level.
