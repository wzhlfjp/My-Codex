# Research Notes

## Scope

Project: "America's Best Law Schools"

Ranking type: U.S. News Best Law Schools rankings. Years are ranking edition years displayed in the source tables.

## Source Hierarchy

Primary target source: U.S. News.

Sources used:

- U.S. News & World Report law school rankings, compiled by George D. Wilson, Stanford Law School Robert Crown Law Library, Research Paper No. 28: 2000-2011.
- U.S. News & World Report law school rankings, compiled by George D. Wilson, Stanford Law School Robert Crown Law Library, Research Paper No. 31: 2012-2013. The same PDF also lists 2014 and was used as a cross-check.
- 7Sage Law School Rankings historical table: 2014-2026. The page describes the table as US News data from 2014 onward.
- U.S. News Best Law Schools current ranking page: current-cycle reference for source identity and spot checks.

## Data Coverage

The normalized animation dataset includes a timeline object for every year 2000-2026.

Every year includes all schools ranked #20 or better. Tied rank groups are kept intact, so some years contain more than 20 rows.

## Animation Data Scope

The animation is ranking-only.

Rows show:

- U.S. News rank
- standardized school name
- movement versus the previous verified ranking year

No bar-length metric, LSAT, GPA, acceptance-rate, employment, bar-passage, tuition, score, or other non-ranking quantitative fields are used in the generated animation dataset.

## Ambiguities And Decisions

- Ties are represented when multiple schools share the same numeric rank in a source table.
- Source tables occasionally omit the repeated rank number on subsequent tied rows. The raw data stores those schools with the shared rank.
- School names are standardized to stable keys such as `uchicago`, `upenn`, `ut-austin`, and `washu`.
- 2014 appears in both the Stanford Law Library compilation and the 7Sage historical table; the generated dataset uses the 7Sage row for continuity with 2014-2026, and the values match the Stanford table at the top-20 level.
- Each year in the 2000-2026 animation timeline displays for 1.5 seconds.
- The source mapping file `data/processed/source_mapping.csv` is generated by the build script and records source IDs and URLs at the school-year-field level.
